Instructions

Please read carefully before starting.

{% if design == 'pairwise' %}
You will complete 10 rounds of pairwise movie comparisons.
1

You will be shown two movies

Each round presents two movies side by side with their title, genre, year, and duration.

2

Choose the one you would prefer to watch

Click the button under the movie you would rather watch. There are no right or wrong answers — go with your genuine preference.

3

Repeat for 10 rounds

After 10 comparisons, you will receive personalized movie recommendations based on your choices.

{% else %}
You will complete 2 ranking tasks, each with 10 movies.
1

You will be shown 10 movies

Each task presents a list of 10 movies with their title, genre, year, and duration.

2

Drag and drop to rank them

Arrange the movies from most preferred (top) to least preferred (bottom) by dragging the items. Go with your genuine preference — there are no right or wrong answers.

3

Repeat for 2 rounds

After 2 ranking tasks, you will receive personalized movie recommendations based on your rankings.

{% endif %}
Important: Please base your choices on your genuine movie preferences, not on what you think is the "correct" answer. The quality of your recommendations depends on honest responses.
